## Coaching points

- Slide the inside knee through the gap and turn the hips as one motion.
- Hand control first, same rule as everything from bottom.
- Finish facing them on your feet — a knee slide that ends on the mat restarts the ride.

## Common errors

- Sliding flat and dragging the trail leg into a leg-ride gift.
- Turning the shoulders before the knee has cleared.

## Drill progressions

- Knee slides down the mat, no partner, 2 lengths.
- Off a blocked stand-up: build → blocked → knee slide, 10 reps.
