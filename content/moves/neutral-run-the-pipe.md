## Coaching points

- Head drives across the body line, hips stay under you — it's a circle, not a push.
- Squeeze the knee to your chest; the leg you hold is dead weight for them.
- Finish through the mat, not onto it — keep driving until the hip touches.

## Common errors

- Head outside or low, eating the front headlock on the finish.
- Straightening the legs and pushing forward instead of circling.

## Drill progressions

- Finish from the caught-single position, 10 per side, defense builds 0 → 75%.
- Chain drill: entry → lift → run the pipe as one rep.

## Situational entries

- Default finish when the leg is at our hip and they whizzer or square up.
