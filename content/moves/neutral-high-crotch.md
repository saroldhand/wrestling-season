## Coaching points

- Hit it off the underhook or a heavy collar tie — it lives off inside control.
- Knee slides to their center line; head stays up and inside.
- Transition immediately: to the double, to the lift, or back out — never stall in it.

## Common errors

- Hanging out under the whizzer instead of transitioning.
- Pulling down on the leg instead of getting your own hips under.

## Drill progressions

- Underhook to high-c entry, 10 per side.
- High-c to double transition on partner's whizzer, 10 reps.

## Situational entries

- Against a heavy collar-tie opponent whose single-leg defense is strong.
