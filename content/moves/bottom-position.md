## Coaching points

- Weight back into the hips, head up, hands ready in front on the whistle.
- Never flat: elbows in, belly off the mat, rebuild the base before anything else.
- Feel where the top pressure is and move opposite the moment it commits.

## Common errors

- Reaching back for the top wrestler instead of building position first.
- Carrying weight forward onto the hands, feeding the chop.

## Drill progressions

- Base rebuilds from flat on the whistle, 3 × 10.
- 15-second survival goes vs escalating top pressure.

## Situational entries

- Every referee's position start; the floor of the whole escape system.
