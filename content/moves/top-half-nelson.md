## Coaching points

- Earn it from the broken-flat position — never chase a half on a based opponent.
- Deep wing before you run it: past the elbow, palm on the skull.
- Run the corner tight; your chest replaces the space you create.

## Common errors

- Shallow half that becomes their whizzer when they fight up.
- Turning them without moving your own hips around the corner.

## Drill progressions

- Half from flat, 5 count to secure back points, 10 reps.
- Breakdown → half chain as one rep, both sides.

## Situational entries

- The finishing move once the breakdown lands; our highest-percentage pin.
