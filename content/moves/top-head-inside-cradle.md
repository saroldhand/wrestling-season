## Coaching points

- The gift when their knee meets their elbow — lock it the beat it happens.
- Lock deep past the knee, head tight, walk the hips in before you tip.
- Squeeze wins cradles: hands locked, elbows in, chest through the lock.

## Common errors

- Locking shallow at the kneecap and having it kicked out.
- Tipping before the lock is closed.

## Drill progressions

- Lock recognition: bottom stands into the knee-to-elbow window, top locks, 10 reps.
- Cradle ride-outs from locked position, 30 seconds to a count.
