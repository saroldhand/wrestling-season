## Coaching points

- Pressure on the near shoulder, knee pulled off the mat — spiral, don't push.
- Head stays on the spiral side; move your feet around the circle with them.
- Convert to the half or the claw the moment they carry to a hip.

## Common errors

- Spiraling with arm strength while kneeling in place.
- Losing the angle and sliding up to a square, high ride.

## Drill progressions

- Spiral to a 3-second knee-off-mat hold, 10 reps.
- Spiral vs stand-up attempt, live from referee's position.

## Situational entries

- Against a strong-based opponent the chop won't move.
