## Coaching points

- Two hands on one: strip the tight waist before you try to stand.
- Elbows stay tight — a lifted elbow is a wing waiting to happen.
- Win the grip fight first; the escape is easy once the hands are cleared.

## Common errors

- Standing into an unbroken lock and getting returned hard.
- Chasing the top hand instead of controlling the wrist you already found.

## Drill progressions

- Grip-strip series from referee's position, 10 reps per grip.
- Hand-fight-only goes: no escape allowed, win = both grips cleared.

## Situational entries

- The first second of every bottom start; under any tight-waist ride.
