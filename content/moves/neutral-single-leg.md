## Coaching points

- Change level before you step — penetration step lands with head up, back flat.
- Head tight to the ribs on the inside; ear pressure, never head outside.
- Lift the leg to your own hip line immediately; don't wrestle on your knees.

## Common errors

- Shooting from the outside without a tie or a reaction to cover the entry.
- Leaving the trail leg behind instead of bringing it up to base.

## Drill progressions

- Penetration steps on the wall line, 2 × 10.
- Entry off collar-tie snap reaction, 10 reps per side, building to 50% defense.

## Situational entries

- Primary attack off any inside-control win; the call when we need a takedown late.
