## Coaching points

- First line of defense: hand to the head or shoulder, hips back one beat.
- Block down, don't reach down — elbow stays bent, weight stays on your feet.
- The down block buys the angle; the snap or re-attack cashes it.

## Common errors

- Reaching past the head and giving up the wrist-deep entry.
- Blocking with straight arms and getting run through anyway.

## Drill progressions

- Down block on shot reaction, partner at 50%, 3 × 10.
- Down block → snap → [[scramble-sprawl-spin]] as one chain rep.

## Situational entries

- Every low-commitment shot from space; the answer that keeps us in offense.
