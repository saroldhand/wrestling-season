## Coaching points

- Shrug the shoulder under, roll across the shoulders — the head never takes weight.
- Catch the arm or the ankle as you come through, or you rolled for nothing.
- Speed beats setup here: hit it as they settle into the ride, not after.

## Common errors

- Rolling on the head/neck — this is why it's gold pod only until it's clean.
- Rolling without capturing anything and giving up the go-behind.

## Drill progressions

- Shoulder rolls down the mat, coach's eyes on neck position every rep.
- Granby from the short-sit vs light hand pressure.

## Situational entries

- Against an over-committed high ride; a surprise weapon, not a base escape.
