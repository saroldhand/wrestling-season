## Coaching points

- A leg ride is a commitment: pick the ankle to the thigh and keep your hips heavy across the body.
- It's a control ride — use it to kill time or set the turn, not to rest.
- Release and rebuild rather than getting stretched out and reversed.

## Common errors

- Riding too high with the ankle and giving up the switch.
- Falling in love with it and stalling out the period.

## Drill progressions

- Ankle pick-up to controlled ride, 5 × :30 holds.
- Escape goes: bottom works stand-up vs the ankle ride.

## Situational entries

- Riding out a lead against a stand-up-heavy opponent.
