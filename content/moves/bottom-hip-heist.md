## Coaching points

- Sit through and replace your hips where theirs were — it's one explosive beat.
- Near arm posts, far leg scissors under; head comes up facing them.
- Hit it off their forward pressure — the heist runs on their momentum.

## Common errors

- Sitting to the hip and pausing, giving up the head-and-arm cover.
- Scissoring the legs without turning the shoulders to face top.

## Drill progressions

- Hip heists down the mat, 2 lengths, no partner.
- Off the stand-up: partner blocks the stand, heist on the block.

## Situational entries

- When the stand-up gets blocked forward; second move in the escape chain.
