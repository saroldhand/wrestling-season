## Coaching points

- Hips down and through their head — the sprawl is an attack, not a retreat.
- Chin control and an elbow pass into the front headlock before they base up.
- Kill the near arm; never let them connect their hands on your leg.

## Common errors

- Sprawling flat and stopping; giving them time to finish on a second effort.
- Chest too high in the front headlock, letting them look up and posture.

## Drill progressions

- Sprawl on the whistle from motion, 3 × 10.
- Shot-defense goes: partner shoots at 50%, sprawl → front headlock → spin.
