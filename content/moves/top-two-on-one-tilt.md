## Coaching points

- Trap the wrist to their belly first; the tilt is wrist control plus hip pop.
- Pull them across your knee line — your hips make the angle, not your arms.
- Hold the near-fall count, rebuild, and run it again — it's a points machine.

## Common errors

- Tilting without the wrist killed, giving up the reversal.
- Chasing the pin and losing the position instead of banking near fall.

## Drill progressions

- Wrist-trap to tilt, 5 clean reps per side.
- 30-second tilt goes: how many near-fall counts can you bank.

## Situational entries

- Up big and building a major; against short, flat-riding opponents.
