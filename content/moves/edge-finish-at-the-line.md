## Coaching points

- Feel the line without looking — eyes stay on the finish, feet read the circle.
- Convert before the edge: speed up the finish the moment the zone is near.
- Steer the finish back to center; a takedown at the boundary must land in bounds.

## Common errors

- Slowing down near the line and letting them step out for the reset.
- Driving straight out of bounds and losing the work.

## Drill progressions

- Shots starting two steps from the line, finish must land in bounds.
- Edge goes: 30 seconds, all action starts at the zone.

## Situational entries

- Late match vs an edge-runner; every tournament mat with a tight circle.
