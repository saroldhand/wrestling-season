## Coaching points

- Chop and drive on the whistle — the first second decides the ride.
- Tight waist pulls their hips to yours; knee replaces their post.
- Break them to their belly at an angle, never straight down the line.

## Common errors

- Chopping the arm without the tight-waist pull, letting them base back up.
- Riding square behind instead of on the hip.

## Drill progressions

- Whistle-start breakdowns, 10 reps, bottom builds 0 → 75%.
- Chop-to-tilt chain, 5 clean reps per side.

## Situational entries

- Default first move on top; the breakdown every turn in our system starts from.
