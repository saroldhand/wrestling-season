## Coaching points

- Inside foot up, heel under the hip — stand into pressure, not away from it.
- Hand control before height: clear the wrist on the near side, then build.
- Cut the corner as you clear — face the official's table, not the wall.

## Common errors

- Standing tall before winning hand control; getting snapped back down.
- Stepping the outside foot first and opening the back door.

## Drill progressions

- Wall stand-ups, 10 reps per side, no partner.
- Partner rides tight waist at 50%, escape clock at :15.

## Situational entries

- Down 1 late in the third, ride-out situation: stand-up is the call.
