## Coaching points

- Elbows inside the knees, hands live at eye level of the opponent's hips.
- Level stays low through motion — move on angles, never straight back.
- Feet never cross, never square: one foot always slightly staggered.
- Reset stance after every exchange; fatigue shows in the hands first.

## Common errors

- Standing up out of stance to circle; getting snapped on the reach.
- Squaring the feet when changing direction.

## Drill progressions

- Mirror drill, 3 × :45, partner leads.
- Motion with level changes on the whistle, no partner.

## Situational entries

- Every neutral restart. This is the base layer of the whole attack system.
