## Coaching points

- Their collar-tie pressure is the fuel: slide the tie by and change your angle.
- Throw the tie past your own shoulder line — half measures just square you up.
- Arrive behind the elbow with your hips already turned to score.

## Common errors

- Sliding the arm without moving the feet; they follow and re-square.
- Forcing it without pressure to redirect.

## Drill progressions

- Slideby off fed collar pressure, 10 per side.
- Combine with [[neutral-snap-and-go]]: snap, they posture, slideby.

## Situational entries

- Late match against a pressure wrestler leaning into every tie.
