## Coaching points

- Hips stay heavy through the spin — lift them and the shot re-fires.
- Spin to the short offense side, hand on the near hip as you arrive.
- Two points is the floor; look up for the trap-arm tilt when they collapse.

## Common errors

- Spinning early while their head still has an angle on your hips.
- Standing up out of the sprawl to spin, giving up the second shot.

## Drill progressions

- Sprawl-spin on the whistle, 3 × 10 alternating sides.
- Continuous shot defense: 45 seconds, score every clean spin.

## Situational entries

- Every defended shot; free points against low-percentage shooters.
