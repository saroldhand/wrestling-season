## Coaching points

- Snap moves their hands to the mat, not just their head down.
- Go on the reaction — spin, shuck, or re-shoot before they rebuild posture.
- Angle off as you snap; a straight-on snap is a stalemate.

## Common errors

- Snapping and admiring it — the score is in the half-second after.
- Pulling with arms only instead of stepping back with the snap.

## Drill progressions

- Snap-spin on the whistle, 3 × 10.
- Snap to front headlock vs a leaning partner, building resistance.

## Situational entries

- Against a head-down, forward-pressure opponent; late-match when they're tired.
