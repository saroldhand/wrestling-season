## Coaching points

- Head presses the near arm down the mat while the far hand rides the wrist.
- It's a lever, not a shove — long pressure until the elbow folds.
- The moment they carry weight to the far side, the bar side opens.

## Common errors

- Levering with the head only, hips floating off the top.
- Chasing it against a strong base instead of switching breakdowns.

## Drill progressions

- Head lever to flat, 10 reps, bottom builds resistance.
- Head lever → [[top-arm-bar]] as one chain rep.

## Situational entries

- Against a tripod base the chop won't move.
