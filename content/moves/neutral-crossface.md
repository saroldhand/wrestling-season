## Coaching points

- The head-inside answer: crossface hard across the jawline, not the neck.
- Weight on the leg being attacked — make the leg heavy before you fight the hands.
- Block the inside arm so they can't climb the leg or re-shoot through.

## Common errors

- Crossfacing while the attacked leg stays light — they finish anyway.
- Letting the inside arm connect to the thigh before the block lands.

## Drill progressions

- Crossface off a fed head-inside single, 10 per side.
- Live defense goes: partner may only attack head inside.
