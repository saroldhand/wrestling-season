## Coaching points

- Hips glued to theirs before they finish standing — ride the hip, not the back.
- Lift with the hips and return at an angle; put them down on their base, not yours.
- Land in the tight waist and restart the breakdown immediately.

## Common errors

- Chasing with arms around the waist while their hips get away.
- Returning straight down and getting rolled through.

## Drill progressions

- Stand-up vs mat return, 10 reps each way — pairs with [[bottom-standup]].
- Lift-and-return technique on a static partner, 5 clean reps.

## Situational entries

- Any time bottom builds to their feet; protecting a lead in the third period.
