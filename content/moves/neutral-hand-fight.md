## Coaching points

- Win inside position: two-on-one wrist or inside collar with elbow down.
- Heavy hands — every touch moves the opponent's head or posture.
- Clear ties you don't want; never wrestle inside someone else's collar tie.

## Common errors

- Reaching without a level change and giving up the underhook.
- Holding a tie without attacking off it.

## Drill progressions

- 1-minute hand-fight goes, score on any clean inside-control win.
- Collar-tie snap to angle, 10 reps per side.

## Situational entries

- First 15 seconds of every neutral exchange; sets up every entry we run.
