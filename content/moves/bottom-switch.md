## Coaching points

- Clear the near arm, sit to the outside hip, reach over their near thigh.
- Hips out THEN back through — the power is in the hip angle, not the arm pull.
- Finish on top in a tight waist; don't settle for the scramble.

## Common errors

- Reaching for the leg without moving the hips out first.
- Switching under a heavy whizzer and getting re-switched.

## Drill progressions

- Switch off the whistle from referee's position, 10 per side.
- Chain: stand-up blocked → switch, 5 reps.

## Situational entries

- When top rides the far side heavy; a two-point answer in a tied match.
