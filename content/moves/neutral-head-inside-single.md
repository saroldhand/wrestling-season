## Coaching points

- Sweep entry: head stays inside and tight, chest up, circle as you connect.
- First finish is circling to the far ankle — chase it until the base breaks.
- Shelf the leg when they whizzer: leg on your hip shelf, posture up.

## Common errors

- Head dropping outside mid-shot and turning it into a bad head-outside single.
- Holding the shelf without an answer while they build a whizzer.

## Drill progressions

- Sweep entries on the fade, 10 per side.
- Chase-the-ankle finish live from the caught position, defense 0 → 75%.

## Situational entries

- Off the shelf: limp arm through, or drop to the [[top-head-inside-cradle]]
  when they kick the leg high.
- Our second core attack alongside [[neutral-single-leg]] — same setups,
  opposite head position.
