## Coaching points

- Never give ground straight back at the line — circle off it, back to center.
- Wrestle the whole zone: a foot on the line is not a surrender.
- Know the count: fleeing calls and step-out tendencies decide close matches.

## Common errors

- Turning to look at the line and eating the shot on the lapse.
- Bailing out of bounds under pressure and handing over the momentum call.

## Drill progressions

- Circle-off-the-line drill: partner pressures, defender must exit along the line.
- Situational goes starting with heels on the line.

## Situational entries

- Against forward-pressure teams that hunt the edge; every close third period.
