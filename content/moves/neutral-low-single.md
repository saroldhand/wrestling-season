## Coaching points

- Ankle-height attack off their weight shift — timing beats depth.
- Hand behind the heel, head up, chest over your own knee.
- Get off the mat immediately: finish up the leg or back out clean.

## Common errors

- Diving from too far out and landing extended.
- Staying under the front headlock instead of bailing when it's late.

## Drill progressions

- Low single timing off partner's step, 10 per side.
- Finish series: run the ankle up to a [[neutral-single-leg]] position.

## Situational entries

- Against tall, heavy-handed opponents whose ankles sit back.
