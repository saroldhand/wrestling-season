## Coaching points

- Lives off the collar tie: lift their elbow with the tie, change level under it.
- Step deep as you duck — the head pops up behind the shoulder, not under the armpit.
- Finish with chest connection behind; two points before they can square.

## Common errors

- Ducking without lifting the elbow first and eating a pancake.
- Ducking wide and surfacing beside them instead of behind.

## Drill progressions

- Duck off the collar-tie clear, 10 per side.
- Chain: snap fake → duck, building speed.

## Situational entries

- Against a heavy collar-tie opponent leaning into the tie.
