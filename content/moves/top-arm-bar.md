## Coaching points

- Bar comes off the broken-flat position — chop first, then hunt the bar.
- Wrist to their back pocket, your chest heavy over the shoulder blade.
- Run the bar and the half together: whichever side they fight, take the other.

## Common errors

- Barring on a based-up opponent and getting rolled through.
- Losing the wrist while turning and giving the arm back.

## Drill progressions

- Bar from flat to a five-count of back points, 10 reps.
- Chop → bar → half chain as one rep, both sides.

## Situational entries

- Second turn in the series when the half is getting defended.
