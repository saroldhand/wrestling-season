## Coaching points

- Built off forward pressure: drive the head to the near hip, lock outside.
- Same rule as head inside — deep lock past the knee or no lock at all.
- Step over the far side to finish; the cradle pins on your terms, not a scramble.

## Common errors

- Cranking the neck without the knee pulled in — no lock, penalty risk.
- Staying behind instead of stepping over to the pinning side.

## Drill progressions

- Lock off the spiral-ride pressure window, 10 reps per side.
- Finish from locked cradle vs live defense, escalating.

## Situational entries

- When they fight the half by carrying their head high and near leg forward.
