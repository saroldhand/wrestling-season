## Coaching points

- Overhook deep and hips IN — a whizzer without hip pressure is a pause button.
- Free hand posts or fights the head; never let them square to your hips.
- Have an exit: limp out, throw-by, or hip down to the front headlock.

## Common errors

- Whizzering with the arm only and running in a circle.
- Holding the whizzer to a stalemate with no second move.

## Drill progressions

- Whizzer hip-pressure holds, 3 × :20 per side.
- Shot → caught → whizzer exit series, 10 reps.

## Situational entries

- Any time our leg is caught; the bridge from defense back to offense.
