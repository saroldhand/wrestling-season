## Coaching points

- Only off a caught single with your leg between theirs — position first, funk second.
- Roll over the shoulder, hunt the far hip, never stop moving to your base.
- Know the bail-out: if the roll dies, concede two and rebuild — never get pinned funky.

## Common errors

- Funking flat onto the back with no far-hip connection.
- Choosing the roll when the boring whizzer would have worked.

## Drill progressions

- Guided funk reps at 25% with a coach calling positions.
- Scramble goes from the caught-single, gold pod only.

## Situational entries

- Elite-shooter matches where straight defense loses; never in a lead-protect situation.
