How installed technique stays sharp: spaced, shuffled review instead of
block review. Every practice file has a `spiral` list — two or three
previously-installed moves that get short, high-quality touches that day.

- **Station format:** 3 stations × 3 minutes inside the first quarter of
  practice, before the day's install.
- **Selection rule:** nothing sits untouched longer than 10 days; the
  dashboard's status rollup and (later) a Dataview query surface what's
  going stale.
- **Status meaning:** a move at `maintenance` lives here permanently; a move
  at `installed` that keeps failing its spiral touches goes back on the
  install plan — that's a coaching signal, not a paperwork problem.

The spiral is why the system stays small: every priority-1 move is a
permanent tenant in this rotation, so forty of them cannot fit. See
[[six-position-systems]].
