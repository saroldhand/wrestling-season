name: Mat circuit
type: conditioning
blocks: [install, volume, refine]

Stations on the mat, 30 seconds each, no rest between: sprawls, stand-ups,
hip heists, spin drill, buddy carries.

- **Run it:** 2–4 rounds.
- Stand-ups and hip heists double as [[bottom-standup]] and [[bottom-hip-heist]] reps.
