**Individual match:** takedown 3, escape 1, reversal 2, near fall 2 (two-count)
or 3 (five-count). The tactical consequences we coach from:

- **Takedown 3 + escape 1 beats reversal 2.** Cutting an opponent to attack
  the takedown is winning math when our neutral game is ahead of theirs —
  see [[edge-finish-at-the-line]] for making the takedown stick.
- **The tilt is a compounding asset.** [[top-two-on-one-tilt]] banking two
  near-fall counts per ride beats hunting one pin against a good bridge.
- **Escape rate is match currency.** A trusted [[bottom-standup]] makes
  every takedown a net +2 exchange; a broken one makes it -3.

**Dual meet:** decision 3, major (8–14) 4, tech fall 5, fall/forfeit/DQ 6.
Bonus points are a team stat — up 7 with a minute left, the right call is
often the tilt for the major, not the risky pin attempt.
