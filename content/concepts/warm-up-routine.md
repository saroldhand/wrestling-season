The standing open of every practice. Mats out, taped, and mopped before the
first whistle — the routine starts on time whether everyone is dressed or
not.

## 5:00–5:20 — movement warm-up

- Jog circle
- Shuffle in / shuffle out
- Forward roll
- Backward roll
- Cartwheel / round off
- Front headspring / handstand
- [[neutral-stance-motion|Stance and motion]]

## 5:20–5:30 — matball

Ten minutes, full effort, winners talk. It's conditioning that doesn't feel
like conditioning — protect it.

It's the first 30 minutes of every practice card ([[2026-11-16|example]]):
schedule times count from the 5:00 whistle, and every block template in
`blocks/` opens with it.
