Referee's position, top at light resistance. On the whistle bottom hits the
called move: [[bottom-standup|stand-up]], [[bottom-hip-heist|hip heist]],
[[bottom-switch|switch]].

- **Run it:** 5 per move per side, then coach calls them in random order.
- **Make it harder:** top resists at 50%; move into the [[escape-clock]].
