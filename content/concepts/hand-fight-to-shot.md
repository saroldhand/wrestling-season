---
id: hand-fight-to-shot
name: Hand fight to a shot
type: drill
---

Twenty seconds of [[neutral-hand-fight|hand fighting]]; the moment you win an
inside tie (see [[tie-taxonomy]]), shoot. Partner resets and you go again.

- **Run it:** 3 × 1:00 per wrestler, partner fights back at 50%.
- **Make it harder:** partner goes 100% on ties, 50% on the shot.
