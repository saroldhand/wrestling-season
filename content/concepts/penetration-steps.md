The four entries under every shot we take. Drilled in warm-ups all season —
footwork before finishes.

1. **Knee over toe** — the standard step; knee tracks over the toe, back
   flat, head up. Under [[neutral-single-leg]] and [[neutral-double-leg]].
2. **High crotch step** — angled entry off the tie; feeds
   [[neutral-high-crotch]].
3. **Double knee drop** — level-change-in-place for close-range shots.
4. **Outside step sweep** — the sweep-single footwork; feeds
   [[neutral-head-inside-single]].

Film: [4 penetration steps — Jeff Jordan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KtIvCxsaFx4)
(pointer only, per the source rules).

Standard progression: wall line reps → on the move → off a tie → under a
live hand fight. Ten clean before fast, every day of [[install]].
