name: Chain wrestling
type: drill
blocks: [install, volume, refine]

Shot, finish, re-attack — three moves linked without a reset, so the second
attack is a habit and not an afterthought.

- **Run it:** 30 seconds on, partner gives a realistic reaction at 50%; switch.
- **Chains to start with:** [[neutral-single-leg]] → [[neutral-run-the-pipe]];
  [[neutral-double-leg]] → cut the corner; sprawled on → [[scramble-sprawl-spin]].
- **Make it harder:** partner picks the reaction; the shooter has to read it.
