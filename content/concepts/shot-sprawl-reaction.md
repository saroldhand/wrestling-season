id: shot-sprawl-reaction
name: Shot / sprawl reaction
type: drill
---

Both in stance, motion on the whistle. Coach calls a side: that wrestler
shoots, the other [[neutral-sprawl-front-headlock|sprawls]] and spins behind.

- **Run it:** 10 calls a round, switch roles, three rounds.
- **Make it harder:** no call — first one to change levels is the shooter.
- Footwork from [[penetration-steps]] on every shot.
