Every move in the vault belongs to one of five frontmatter positions —
**neutral, top, bottom, scramble, edge** — but the room thinks in six
systems, because neutral is really two jobs:

1. **Neutral offense** — [[neutral-stance-motion]] into ties, entries, and
   finishes. Score first.
2. **Neutral defense** — [[neutral-sprawl-front-headlock]] and re-attacks.
   Defense that ends in our points.
3. **Top** — breakdown, ride, turn: [[top-tight-waist-arm-chop]] into
   [[top-half-nelson]].
4. **Bottom** — base, hand fight, escape: [[bottom-position]] into
   [[bottom-standup]].
5. **Scramble** — the connective tissue: [[scramble-whizzer]] first, funk
   only where it's earned.
6. **Edge** — the boundary is a position, not an interruption:
   [[edge-finish-at-the-line]].

The `/system` board groups by the five frontmatter positions; this concept
is the coaching frame on top of them. A wrestler is "system-complete" when
they have one trusted answer in all six.
