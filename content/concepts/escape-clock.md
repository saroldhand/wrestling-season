The bottom-system pressure drill. Referee's position, top rides honest,
bottom has a fixed clock to escape — score or the rep fails.

- **Clock:** :15 to start the season, tightening to :10 by [[refine]].
- **Sets:** 5 reps per wrestler per side, both partners log makes/misses.
- **Rules:** escape or reversal counts; a stalemate or a ride-out is a miss.
  Top wrestler is drilling too — [[top-mat-return]] on every stand attempt.
- **Progression:** blue pod starts at 25% top resistance; gold rides live.

The team make-rate is the bottom-system KPI the [[install]] and [[volume]]
blocks track week over week. Run it every session that touches
[[bottom-standup]], [[bottom-hip-heist]], or [[bottom-switch]].
