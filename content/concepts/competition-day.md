The same day, every time — duals and tournaments differ only in repetition.

## Before

- Weigh-in bag packed the night before; skin-check paperwork with the coach.
- Dynamic warm-up 40 minutes out: stance and motion, level changes, hip
  heists down the mat, partner spin drill. Sweating before match one.
- Two hard hand-fight goes ([[neutral-hand-fight]]) to arrive in contact.

## Between matches

- Cool down, dry clothes, fuel, film note within 10 minutes — one thing
  that worked, one thing to fix, written in the wrestler's own words.
- No new technique between matches. Corrections come from the fix list.

## After

- Team film assignment logged against the practice file's
  `## After-practice notes` the same night.
- Bumps and bruises reported before leaving the gym — injury notes go to
  `private/`, never in match commentary.
