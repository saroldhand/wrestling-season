name: Breakdown to turn
type: drill
blocks: [install, volume, refine]
---

From the top of referee's position: break them down with
[[top-tight-waist-arm-chop|tight waist and chop]], then turn with the
[[top-half-nelson|half]].

- **Run it:** 5 each side, bottom gives honest base but no bridge.
- **Make it harder:** bottom fights the half — top has to keep the turn.
