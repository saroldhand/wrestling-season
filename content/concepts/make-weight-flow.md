Friday work: active and continuous at a moderate pace — drilling,
[[shadow-wrestling]], jogging, stance and motion — so wrestlers sweat
without crushing their legs before Saturday.

- **Run it:** 15 minutes continuous, then the weight check.
- No rubber or plastic suits (NFHS rules ban them). Descent plans live in
  `private/`.
