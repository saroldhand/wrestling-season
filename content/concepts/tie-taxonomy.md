The shared names for every tie, so a correction shouted across the room
means the same thing to everyone. Winning these positions is the job of
[[neutral-hand-fight]]; the Kyle Snyder inside-control film is attached
there.

- **Inside tie** — one hand inside the elbow, thumb up.
- **Double inside tie** — both hands inside; the position our offense wants.
- **Collar tie** — hand behind the head, elbow down and heavy.
- **Over tie** — arm over their collar tie, killing it.
- **Wrist control** — two-on-one or cross-wrist; every strip starts here.
- **Underhook** — arm under theirs, chest connected; feeds the
  [[neutral-high-crotch]].
- **Overhook** — arm over theirs; the [[scramble-whizzer]] is the deep version.
- **Double over** — both arms over; a pinning-position tie, not a stalling one.
- **Double under** — both arms under; finish or throw territory, never a rest stop.
