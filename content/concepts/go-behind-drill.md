id: go-behind-drill
name: Sprawl and go-behind
type: drill
blocks: [install, volume]
---

Partner shoots at 50%; [[neutral-sprawl-front-headlock|sprawl]], take the
front headlock, and [[scramble-sprawl-spin|spin behind]] for the takedown.

- **Run it:** 10 reps, switch, alternate directions.
- **Make it harder:** shooter tries to come up — finish the go-behind anyway.
